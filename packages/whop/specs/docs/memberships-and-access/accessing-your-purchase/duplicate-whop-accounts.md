# Duplicate Whop accounts

> What to do if you have more than one Whop account

Having more than one Whop account can make a purchase look missing. The membership might belong to a different account than the one you're using.

## Why you might have multiple Whop accounts

* You signed up or made a purchase using different emails, such as personal and work addresses
* You used "Continue with Google" on one device and email/password on another
* You signed up or logged in with iCloud or Discord credentials, and it created you a new account
* You created a new account instead of logging into an existing one
* You made a purchase with Apple Pay, and you don't know the email used to complete that purchase

## What to do

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the email address you used to create your Whop accounts" titleSize="h3">
    Read [How to find your purchase](/memberships-and-access/accessing-your-purchase/how-to-find-your-purchase) to track down the different email addresses you used to make your different accounts.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose which account you would like to use moving forward" titleSize="h3">
    Pick the single Whop account you want to use for all your memberships.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Follow the membership transfer guide to consolidate your accounts" titleSize="h3">
    See [Transfer a membership](/memberships-and-access/accessing-your-purchase/transfer-a-membership) for the full steps.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Avoid creating new accounts for future purchases. Always log in to the account you use for your memberships.
