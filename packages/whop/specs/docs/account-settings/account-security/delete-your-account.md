# Delete your account

> Permanently delete your Whop user account from account settings.

This process deletes your **entire Whop user account**. It doesn't delete just one business or whop. If you only want to remove one of your businesses, see [Manage products](/manage-your-business/products/manage-products) for options.

## How to delete your entire Whop account

1. Sign in to your account on Whop.
2. Go to [Account settings](https://whop.com/@me/settings/).
3. Select the **Danger zone** tab.
4. Select **Delete account**. A form will appear.
5. Type **delete** into the text box and select **Delete account**.

<Warning>
  Cancel all memberships before you delete your Whop account. See
  [Cancel a
  subscription](/memberships-and-access/cancellations-and-refunds/cancel-a-subscription)
  to learn how to cancel a membership.
</Warning>
