# Hide approximate location

> Turn your approximate location, visible to other users, on or off from account settings.

You can control whether your approximate location is visible to other users on Whop. Turn the setting off to hide it.

## How to hide your approximate location

1. Sign in to your account on Whop.
2. Go to [Account settings](https://whop.com/@me/settings/).
3. Select the **General** tab.
4. Scroll to the bottom of the page and find the **Show approximate location** setting.
5. Turn the switch on the right to the off position.
