# Access higher checkout links

> Apply to create checkout links over $2,500 and accept higher-priced offers

When you first join Whop, Whop caps checkout links at \$2,500. If your offer is more than \$2,500, you need to apply to access higher checkout links.

Since higher payments come with additional risk, Whop needs more information about your business and what you're selling.

## How to apply for higher checkout links

You can apply either directly from the [Checkout link setup page](/manage-your-business/payment-processing/create-checkout-link), or from **Settings** > **Payments** ([dashboard settings](https://whop.com/dashboard/settings/payments/)) on your dashboard.

The application requests the following information:

* Your highest-priced offer and what customers receive for that price
* Your history of successfully selling offers at this price point

<Note>Whop reviews and processes applications within 24 hours.</Note>

## How to track the status of your application

After making a decision, Whop sends the original account owner an email, Whop DM, and notification on the **Payouts** page. Whop contacts you if the application needs more information.

Once approved, you will immediately be able to create higher checkout links.

## How to access higher checkout links without business history

If your business is new, you might not have processing history for Whop to review. You can still access higher checkout links by holding a percentage of your funds in reserve for 60 days.

With this option, you can start collecting higher-priced payments while keeping enough funds in your Whop balance to cover disputes or refund requests.

[Contact Whop Support](https://whop.com/support) if your new business needs a reserve to access checkout links over \$2,500 in United States dollars.
